Professional Work

This page showcases websites, digital platforms, and other projects I have contributed to throughout my professional experience in higher education and healthcare. My current work focuses on the QPSi website and QPSi Knowledgebase, which I continue to maintain and develop. My previous projects include the UF Office of Real Estate website and ConneQtor QMS. I am no longer responsible for maintaining those projects, so any outdated content, errors, or formatting issues on those sites may reflect changes made after my departure. Access note: ConneQtor QMS is restricted to users with a UF GatorLink account who are either on-site at a UF Health location or connected through the UF VPN.

Ongoing Projects

UF College of Medicine's QPSi Website

The website for the UF College of Medicine's Quality and Patient Safety Initiative (QPSi), sharing information about its programs, educational opportunities, resources, and quality improvement work.

I manage website content, page layouts, graphics, and ongoing UX improvements, while supporting accessibility, SEO, and analytics across QPSi's digital platforms.

Visit the QPSi Website

QPSi Knowledgebase

A digital learning and resource platform developed by QPSi Academy and Outreach in conjunction with the UF College of Education's E-learning, Technology, and Communications (ETC) team to help healthcare professionals explore quality improvement through educational materials, practical tools, and clinical resources.

I contribute to platform development, UX design, branding, and visual assets, including banners. I also monitor analytics and continue to support ongoing website improvements.

Visit the QPSi Knowledgebase Website

Previous Projects

UF Office of Real Estate Website

The University of Florida Office of Real Estate website, providing information about university properties, real estate services, leasing, and available resources.

I managed the website's WordPress development and administration, including its migration from Terminalfour, content updates, SEO improvements, and analytics-informed changes that increased website traffic by 240%.

Visit the UF Office of Real Estate Website

UF Health ConneQtor QMS

A quality management platform that enables UF-affiliated healthcare professionals to register, manage, and track quality improvement projects.

I provided UX consultation to support the platform's usability, interface design, and overall user experience. Platform access requires a UF GatorLink account and VPN access to load.
