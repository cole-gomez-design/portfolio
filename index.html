About Me

I'm a web designer and digital experience specialist with a background in higher education, educational technology, and digital content development. My work focuses on creating accessible, user-friendly websites and digital platforms that make information easier to navigate and use. I've worked on website design, development, and UX improvements across the University of Florida, including projects for the College of Medicine and Office of Real Estate. I'm particularly interested in the intersection of web design, user experience, and educational technology, and how thoughtful digital design can improve access to information and learning.

QPSi Knowledgebase

A digital learning and resource platform developed by QPSi Academy and Outreach in conjunction with the UF College of Education's E-learning, Technology, and Communications (ETC) team to help healthcare professionals explore quality improvement through educational materials, practical tools, and clinical resources.

I contribute to platform development, UX design, branding, and visual assets, including banners. I also monitor analytics and continue to support ongoing website improvements.

UF Office of Real Estate Website

The University of Florida Office of Real Estate website, providing information about university properties, real estate services, leasing, and available resources.

I managed the website's WordPress development and administration, including its migration from Terminalfour, content updates, SEO improvements, and analytics-informed changes that increased website traffic by 240%.
